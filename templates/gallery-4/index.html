Questions We Hear

Five answers, written plainly.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Mostly growing companies with 10 to 500 people: food and drink brands, shops, clinics, charities, tech start-ups and the odd festival. What they share is a founder or marketing lead who wants bold work and honest numbers.

How much does a project cost?

Brand identities usually start around $18,000, websites around $15,000 and monthly marketing retainers around $5,000. We send a fixed quote after our first call, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

Who will actually work on my project?

The people you meet in the first meeting. Every client gets a senior lead, a strategist and the makers the job needs. We never hand the work to a different team once the contract is signed.

Do you only take on big projects?

No. Some clients start with a single campaign, a product film or three months of social content. If it goes well, the work tends to grow from there. We just need a clear goal and someone who can make decisions.

How do we get started?

Send a short note about what you are building and what is getting in the way. We reply within two working days and set up a free 30 minute call, with no pitch deck and no pressure.
