Meet Mosswick Studio

Mosswick started in 2014 when three people sharing a desk above a bike shop got tired of watching good companies hide behind forgettable logos and dull campaigns.

We're thirty-two strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers and media buyers in one studio. The person shooting your film hears the strategy first hand, and the person buying your ads knows why the brand looks the way it does.

What we make

Mosswick Studio is a creative and marketing studio in Lowmere, led by founder and creative director Ines Calloway. Its 32 people make brands, websites, films and campaigns: brand strategy and identity, websites, monthly social content, film and motion, and paid search and social, for clients in food and drink, hospitality, culture and retail.

Pretty work that sells nothing is just expensive decoration. Every brand needs one idea it can say in a single breath.
