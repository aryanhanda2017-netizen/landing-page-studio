What we make

Mosswick Studio is a creative and marketing studio in Lowmere. Since 2014, thirty-two strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers, developers and media buyers in one studio have made brands, packaging, websites, films and campaigns for food brands, shops, clinics and city charities, with marketing that earns its keep. Most clients stay for years.

Where we began

Mosswick started in 2014 as three people sharing a desk above a bike shop. We were tired of watching good companies hide behind forgettable logos and campaigns nobody could recall a week later. Today the person shooting your film hears the strategy first hand, and the person buying your ads knows why the brand looks the way it does.

7Floor 7 · Questions

Questions people ask us

01

What kind of companies do you work with?

Mostly growing companies with 10 to 500 people: food and drink brands, shops, clinics, charities, tech start-ups and the odd festival.

02

How much does a project cost?

Brand identities usually start around $18,000, websites around $15,000 and retainers around $5,000. You get a fixed quote after our first call.

03

How long does a brand identity take?

Most identity projects take eight to twelve weeks from the first workshop to final files. We agree the timeline in writing before we start.

04

Do you only take on big projects?

No. Some clients start with a single campaign, a product film or three months of social content. If it goes well, the work tends to grow.

05

Who will actually work on my project?

The people you meet in the first meeting: a senior lead, a strategist and the makers the job needs. We never hand the work to another team.

06

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes, and we often do. We can lead the creative and hand over files and guidelines, or join your team's weekly rhythm. You own everything we make.

07

Do you run ads as well as make them?

Yes. Our performance team plans, buys and tests paid search and social, and you see spend and results in a live dashboard, updated daily.

08

How do you measure success?

We agree two or three numbers at the start, such as enquiries, sales or sign-ups, and report on them every month in plain language. If something is not working, we say so early.
