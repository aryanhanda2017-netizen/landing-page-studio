      Mosswick at a glance

      Mosswick is a creative and marketing studio in Lowmere. Its 32 strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers and media buyers work in one studio on brand strategy, identity, websites, social content, film, campaigns and paid media, for food brands, shops, clinics, charities and tech start-ups. It has launched more than 140 brands since 2014.
