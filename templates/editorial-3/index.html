Stillreed · product films and launch

A mostly silent campaign for headphones

Stillreed’s headphones are about silence, so the campaign is mostly silent. We filmed them in empty pools, libraries and night trains, then let the sound fall away. Pre-orders hit target in 11 days.

On set, then the edit back at the studio in Lowmere

Tallow Lane · packs

One roaster, twelve bags, one system

Tallow Lane had great coffee and twelve different bag designs. We built one system: a bold condensed wordmark, a colour for each origin and a tasting scale anyone can read. Wholesale accounts went from 19 to 47.

Decide.

Five briefs, each cut down to one problem worth solving.

Okra & Ash · opening

Opening night, fully booked

A wood-fired restaurant with eight weeks to open and no name on the door. An identity built around the fire, heavy card menus and six weeks of social before the doors opened.

  • 5 weeksbooked out from the first night

Velloway · launch film

Outerwear built for wet commutes

Its old ads showed mountain tops. We shot riders on city streets at dusk in real drizzle: a 60 second spot and twenty short cuts. Online sales rose 38% in launch month.

Marshlight · tickets

Festival posters people keep, and a sell-out nine days early

A look built from reeds, mist and lantern light, screen-printed posters and short films of the site at dawn.

Gearfinch · launch and website

Cargo e-bikes for school runs and hills

Real families filmed on real school runs, and a website where parents can book a free test ride at home.

Fernstride · campaign and social

Running shoes for the rest of us

Most running ads show elite athletes. We worked with local run clubs, filmed slow Sunday miles and wrote about getting out the door. Brand searches rose 52% in twelve weeks.

Make.

Design, writing and film, made together and checked weekly.

Houndsbury · packs

Dog food you can see into

Fresh dog meals, shipped weekly. Every pouch got a clear window onto the real food, and paid social ran short videos of dogs at dinner time, tested against price-led ads.

  • 2.4xsubscriptions in a year
  • Weeklyad tests against price-led ads

Marlo Rue · lookbook and film

Knitwear filmed like a slow film, on a sheep farm

The winter range, shot on a working sheep farm over two grey days and edited like a short film, scored with needles and wind. It sold through in five weeks.

Cairnstone · brand film

Gear for the long way round

Four days in the hills with a small crew, filming the pack in rain, mud and early starts. Sell-through ran 31% above forecast.

Launch.

Websites, shops and packs, shipped and watched closely in the first weeks.

The Larchway · identity and website

A hotel that feels like a weekend

A 30-room hotel in a timber warehouse by the canal. The brand comes from the building, and the new site sells rooms by mood. Direct bookings: 22% to 58%.

Out the door

A quote site with no small print, and packs and shops for brands just starting out.

Who started it
