Four practices, one team, one roof

Mosswick is a creative and marketing studio in Lowmere. Thirty-two strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers and media buyers work in one studio, making brands that look good and marketing that earns its keep, across four practices: Strategy, Branding, Content and Digital.

Strategy

We find what makes you worth choosing and write it down plainly.

  • Positioning and naming
  • Audience research
  • Brand voice and messaging

Branding

Logos, type, colour and rules that make you recognisable at a glance.

  • Logo and wordmark
  • Colour and type system
  • Packaging and labels

Content

Posts, reels and brand films that people stop scrolling for.

  • Brand films and product videos
  • Short-form video and reels
  • A monthly social calendar

Digital

Fast, clear websites and paid media run by people who know your brand.

  • Websites and online shops
  • Paid search and paid social
  • A live dashboard and monthly review

Film & motion

Cairnstone: gear for the long way round

For Cairnstone's first rucksack we took a small crew into the hills for four days and filmed the pack doing its job: rain, mud and very early starts. The film anchors a campaign across magazines, feeds and shops.

We also make product films for Stillreed and a slow knitwear film for Marlo Rue.

Campaigns

Festival posters people want to keep

Identity, posters and a ticket campaign for Marshlight Festival, built from reeds, mist and lantern light. The posters sold out at the merch tent, and the tickets sold out nine days early.

Print & posters

Covers, posters and press ads

Book covers for Quillmoor Press, laundrette posters for Owlhour's night school and press ads for the Stillreed headphone launch.

Social content

Launch films for Fizzwold's new cans, a monthly try-on series for Periwink's eyewear shops and six weeks of opening posts for Okra & Ash.
