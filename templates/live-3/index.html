      Questions · before you ask

      Frequently asked questions

      What kind of companies do you work with?

      Mostly growing companies with 10 to 500 people: food and drink brands, shops, clinics, charities, tech start-ups and the odd festival. What they share is a founder or marketing lead who wants bold work and honest numbers.

      How much does a project cost?

      Brand identities usually start around $18,000, websites around $15,000 and monthly marketing retainers around $5,000. We send a fixed quote after our first call, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

      How long does a brand identity take?

      Most identity projects take eight to twelve weeks from the first workshop to final files. A full rebrand with a website and launch campaign usually runs four to six months. We agree the timeline in writing before we start.

      Do you only take on big projects?

      No. Some clients start with a single campaign, a product film or three months of social content. If it goes well, the work tends to grow from there. We just need a clear goal and someone who can make decisions.

      Who will actually work on my project?

      The people you meet in the first meeting. Every client gets a senior lead, a strategist and the makers the job needs. We never hand the work to a different team once the contract is signed.

      Can you work alongside our in-house team?

      Yes, and we often do. We can lead the creative and hand over files and guidelines, or join your team's weekly rhythm for campaigns and content. Either way, you own everything we make for you.

      Do you run ads as well as make them?

      Yes. Our performance team plans, buys and tests paid search and social, and sits in the same room as the people who make the ads. You see spend and results in a live dashboard, updated daily.

      How do you measure success?

      We agree two or three numbers at the start, such as enquiries, sales or sign-ups, and report on them every month in plain language. If something is not working, we say so early and change course.

      Can we work together if you are not in Lowmere?

      Yes. About half of our clients are in other cities and a few are overseas. We run most meetings on video and travel for workshops and shoots whenever being in the room helps the work.

      How do we get started?

      Send a short note about what you are building and what is getting in the way. We reply within two working days and set up a free 30 minute call, with no pitch deck and no pressure.
