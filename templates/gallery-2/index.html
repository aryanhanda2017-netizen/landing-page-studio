What we make

Mosswick Studio is a creative and marketing studio of 32 people in Lowmere. Strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers and media buyers work on the same briefs, for food and drink brands, shops, clinics, charities and tech start-ups that would rather be remembered.

One studio, in an old print works in Lowmere, since 2014.

  1. Brand strategy

    Workshops, customer interviews and a hard look at your market, then a positioning and a voice.

  2. Brand identity

    Logo, colour, type and guidelines that work from a favicon to a building wrap.

  3. Packaging

    Pack structure, range design, labels and boxes for the shelf and the doorstep, with your printers in from day one.

  4. Campaigns

    One idea carried across outdoor, social, print and screen: concept, key visuals, copywriting, media plan and launch assets.

  5. Websites

    Fast, clear websites, designed and built in one team.

  6. Performance marketing

    Tracking first, then paid search and social with weekly creative tests and a live dashboard.
