What Mosswick does

Mosswick is a creative and marketing studio in Lowmere. Thirty-two strategists, designers, writers, filmmakers and media buyers make brands, websites, films and campaigns. Case studies

Velloway

Rainproof jackets · film, campaign, paid media

Velloway makes jackets for people who cycle to work in the rain, but its old ads showed mountain tops. We shot on city streets at dusk in real drizzle, then cut one film into a 60 second spot and twenty short edits.

  • 38%Online sales up, launch month
  • 60sHero spot from one shoot
  • 20Short cuts for social and ads
Read the Velloway case

Fernstride

Running shoes for beginners · campaign, social and film

Most running ads show elite athletes, and Fernstride wanted people who had only just started. We filmed slow Sunday miles with local run clubs and wrote lines about the hardest part: getting out the door.

  • 52%Brand searches up
  • 12 wksOn social and bus shelters
Read the Fernstride case

Tallow Lane

Coffee roaster with four cafes · identity and packaging

Tallow Lane had great coffee and twelve different bag designs. We built one system: a bold condensed wordmark, a colour for each origin and a tasting scale anyone can read, plus new cups, menus and signs for the cafes.

  • 47Wholesale accounts, up from 19
  • 12 to 1Bag designs into one system
Read the Tallow Lane case

Okra & Ash

Wood-fired restaurant · brand, site and social

Eight weeks to open and no name on the door. We built the brand around the fire and ran a behind-the-scenes series for six weeks before opening night.

  • 8 wksFrom no name to open doors
  • 5 wksBooked out after opening
Read the Okra & Ash case

Shelterly

Home insurance start-up · brand, website and paid search

Shelterly writes policies in plain English and pays claims fast. We gave it calm colours and short sentences, and the new site cut the quote form from 22 questions to 9.

  • 64%More quotes completed
  • 22 to 9Questions on the quote form
Read the Shelterly case

The Larchway

30-room boutique hotel · identity, website and paid media

A hotel in a converted timber warehouse by the canal. We built the brand from the building, and the new site sells rooms by mood.

  • 58%Direct bookings, up from 22%
  • 30Rooms, sold by mood
